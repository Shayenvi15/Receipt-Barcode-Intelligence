What it does

Two complementary processing pipelines handle receipts and product barcodes through a unified FastAPI interface.

01 — RECEIPT INTELLIGENCE

OCR & Structured Receipt Extraction

Receipt images are processed through OCR using Tesseract. The extracted text is then interpreted by Gemini and validated against a structured Pydantic schema to produce consistent receipt information.

02 — BARCODE INTELLIGENCE

Barcode & QR Decoding

Barcode and QR images are processed using pyzbar. The implementation supports EAN13, UPC-A, CODE128 and QR codes.

03 — PRODUCT LOOKUP

AI-Assisted Product Identification

Decoded commercial barcodes can be passed to Gemini for product identification, with Google Search grounding used to retrieve relevant product information.

04 — STRUCTURED OUTPUT

Validated JSON Responses

Receipt information is represented through Pydantic models, allowing the application to validate structured transaction and item data before returning the API response.

System Architecture

The application separates visual processing, AI interpretation and API delivery into modular components.

Receipt Pipeline

Receipt Image → Image Processing → Tesseract OCR → Gemini → Pydantic Validation → Structured Receipt JSON

Barcode Pipeline

Barcode / QR Image → pyzbar Decoder → Barcode Identification → Gemini Product Lookup → Structured Product Information
